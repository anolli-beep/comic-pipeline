countries.geojson: country outlines from Natural Earth (1:50m admin 0 countries), public domain,
https://www.naturalearthdata.com. Coordinates rounded to 0.01 degrees to keep the file small.
Some areas are kept as their own shapes so maps follow internationally recognised borders and can be
marked separately: "Crimea" (taken out of Natural Earth's Russia shape; coloured with Ukraine unless a
plan colours it on its own), "Golan Heights" (taken out of Natural Earth's Israel shape along an
approximate line; coloured with Syria unless a plan colours it on its own), and Natural Earth's
"Palestine" split into "West Bank" and "Gaza Strip".
